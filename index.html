SUPPORT METHODOLOGY

How I Approach Troubleshooting

A structured troubleshooting process helps identify root causes while keeping users informed and minimizing downtime.

01

Identify

Gather symptoms, error messages, affected systems, and relevant user information.

02

Diagnose

Isolate the problem using appropriate system, network, and application diagnostics.

03

Troubleshoot

Apply appropriate remediation while following a systematic troubleshooting process.

04

Verify

Confirm that the issue is resolved and that the system is functioning as expected.

05

Document

Record symptoms, troubleshooting actions, findings, and resolution details.

06

Follow Up

Confirm the user can resume normal work and identify any remaining issues requiring escalation.
