TROUBLESHOOTING APPROACH

How I Approach Technical Issues

01

Identify

Gather symptoms, error messages, system information, and user-impact details.

02

Diagnose

Use appropriate diagnostic tools and command-line utilities to isolate the likely cause.

03

Troubleshoot

Apply the least disruptive remediation appropriate for the issue.

04

Validate

Test the system after remediation and confirm that expected functionality has been restored.

05

Document

Record the troubleshooting steps, results, and relevant configuration information.

06

Escalate

Identify situations requiring additional expertise and document the information needed for effective escalation.
