LAB OBJECTIVE

Maintaining & Optimizing Endpoints

This hands-on project focused on diagnosing computer hardware and storage issues and applying targeted maintenance to improve endpoint reliability and performance.

The work emphasized identifying the affected component, determining whether repair or replacement was appropriate, completing the hardware or storage change, and verifying system operation afterward.

DIAGNOSTICS

Hardware Troubleshooting Process

Hardware maintenance was approached using the same structured troubleshooting principles used for software and network incidents.

01

Identify Symptoms

Identify performance problems, storage limitations, hardware behavior, or other endpoint symptoms.

02

Inspect Hardware

Examine the affected hardware and determine whether the issue is related to a physical component or configuration.

03

Determine Repair

Evaluate whether maintenance, component replacement, configuration, or upgrade is the appropriate solution.

04

Perform Maintenance

Complete the required hardware or storage work using an organized and repeatable process.

05

Verify Operation

Confirm the endpoint boots correctly and that the upgraded or repaired component operates as expected.

06

Document Results

Record the work performed and the resulting condition of the endpoint for future reference.

ENDPOINT SUPPORT

From Hardware Repair to User Productivity

Endpoint hardware problems can directly affect a user's ability to perform daily work. Slow storage, insufficient capacity, or failing components can appear as application performance or general system reliability problems.

This lab provided hands-on practice identifying physical endpoint issues and selecting targeted solutions rather than automatically replacing an entire system.
